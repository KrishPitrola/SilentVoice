Why lip reading is hard
These sounds look completely identical
No camera resolution helps here — /p/, /b/, and /m/ all produce the exact same complete lip closure. A human lip reader must infer the correct sound from context alone. This is precisely why SilentVoice doesn't stop at the VSR model: DistilBERT reads the predicted sequence and resolves ambiguity the way a brain does.
